Our Story

Every great story
begins unexpectedly.

Ours started with a single fragrance. A few years ago, we stepped into a small perfume boutique that specialized in crafting luxury and niche fragrances. We weren't looking to build a brand—we were simply curious. One fragrance changed everything.

It wasn't just the scent that stayed with us. It was the emotion it carried, the memories it awakened, and the realization that something invisible could leave such a lasting impression. That moment sparked an obsession that became 224 Studio.

Today, every fragrance we create is thoughtfully developed for modern sensibilities — precise, intentional, and made to leave a lasting impression long after the moment has passed.

Our Philosophy

Simplicity is
the highest form of luxury.

We believe fragrance is identity. Not an accessory — a signature. The way you smell is the first thing remembered and the last thing forgotten.

CITRINE was built on a single conviction: that brightness, warmth, and depth are not opposites. They are three movements of a single, unbroken thought. No excess. No noise. Just intention, refined to its most essential form.

Modern perfumery, at its finest, does not compete for attention — it earns it. Quietly. Permanently.
